Key business insights

  • 01West and East together account for 61% of total revenue ($1.38M), while the South contributes just 17.2% — a $321K gap vs the West alone.
  • 02RFM analysis shows 305 customers (38.3%) are At Risk or Lost, carrying 37.1% of total revenue — the biggest retention opportunity in the dataset.
  • 03The South has zero sales of Canon imageCLASS 2200 Copier despite $44K revenue in East+West — the cleanest whitespace product opportunity found.
  • 04Central region grew 42.2% in 2017 then declined 2.78% in 2018 — the only region showing contraction, needs root cause investigation.
  • 05Shipping speed has near-zero correlation (-0.007) with order value — faster delivery alone won't increase basket size.
